## Homeless Family Services

When a family is experiencing homelessness, Our Family Services is a point of entry for them to launch their fresh start. They partner with **30 other community programs** to get families housed — and the strength of the program is keeping them housed.

### Tools for Self-Sufficiency

- Intensive case management
- Work and education assistance
- Counseling
- Life and parenting skills
- All designed for long-term self-sufficiency and to ensure clients never experience homelessness again.

### Impact (FY23)

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| Adults & children housed | **935** (in 313 households) |
| Individuals assessed for Coordinated Entry | **5,865** |

### Housing Programs

Our Family Services' Housing programs provide **emergency shelter and rapid re-housing** for families experiencing homelessness. Each program includes:

- Intensive case management
- Job search guidance
- Apartments of the client's choosing throughout the community
- Family goal plans with short- and long-term objectives and timelines for overcoming barriers to self-sufficiency
- Limited funds to help low-income families avoid homelessness

### Coordinated Entry & VI-SPDAT

Our Family participates in the community-wide **Coordinated Entry** assessment and referral system to fill openings in their homeless housing programs. The system prioritizes completing **VI-SPDAT** (Vulnerability Index – Service Prioritization Decision Assistance Tool) assessments for households that:

- Are **literally homeless** (on streets, in vehicles, at emergency shelter, etc.), or
- Are in a **dangerous situation** (e.g., trafficking, high-risk domestic violence, etc.)

Such households complete the VI-SPDAT assessment application tool and the HMIS system consent form.

### Reunion House & Safe Place (Youth ages 12–17)

**Reunion House Youth & Family Support Program** offers 24/7 supportive housing and resources for any young person ages 12–17 who needs a safe place to stay. As **the only program of its kind in Southern Arizona**, Reunion House provides a comfortable, no-cost environment where youth can access immediate support, any time of day or night.

The team works alongside young people to explore their goals and connect with resources that matter to them. Offerings include:

- Safe, supportive housing with caring staff available 24/7
- Transportation to school and appointments
- Nutritious meals and essential items
- Educational support
- Family support services
- Connections to community resources
- Flexible stays — 100% voluntary — State licensed

**Reunion House is a Safe Place.** No insurance or payment is required.

As **Pima County's designated Safe Place agency**, Our Family partners with local businesses including libraries, QuikTrip stations, and other community locations where young people can connect with immediate assistance 24/7. Many young people find their way to Reunion House through the National Safe Place network, but youth are welcomed through any path that feels accessible.

### Services for Young Adults (Ages 18–24)

More than **700 homeless young adults** between ages 18–24 sought housing at Our Family and through the local coordinated entry system last year.

- **1 in 3** were pregnant or parenting
- Many had experienced **sex or labor trafficking**
- **Nearly half** were sleeping outdoors, in abandoned buildings, or other dangerous and unsafe places — at high risk for violence, human trafficking, and sexual exploitation

#### Unbroken Pathway from Homelessness to Self-Sufficiency

- Street and community-based outreach
- Emergency shelter
- Variety of supportive housing programs (6 months to 2 years or more)

#### Specialized Housing Services

- Homeless LGBTQ young adults
- Teen parents
- Sex and labor trafficked young adults
- Young adults with disabilities

#### Wrap-Around Services

National studies find that homeless young adults require much more than housing. Each program includes:

- Education and employment connections
- Independent living skills
- Behavioral and physical health care
- Legal services
- On-site counseling
- Life and parenting skills classes
- A case manager — a caring adult who helps identify personal goals, address barriers, and begin the journey out of homelessness

#### Diversion Funds

Diversion funds are available to help young adults through age 24 find alternatives to shelter and prevent homelessness.

## Family and Youth Homeless **Prevention** Funding

Our Family Services has been awarded **Family and Youth Homeless Prevention funding**. The purpose of this program is to **keep clients housed in their current rental unit** — i.e., prevent eviction before it happens, not rehouse after.

### Who Can Be Served

- **Housed families with minor children** (any age head-of-household)
- **Housed individual youth aged 18–24**
- Folks at **imminent risk** due to unforeseen financial situations or with **late notices**

### Who Cannot Be Served

- Anyone with an **eviction notice**, **court date**, or who is **already locked out** of their unit

### Eligibility Requirements

- Verifiable income at or **below 80% AMI** (cash benefits or employment income)
- Must be on a **lease agreement** with their own name on it
- Must provide landlord info, including the **lease** and **W-9**

### Funds Cannot Cover

- Furniture
- Moving / relocation / rehousing costs
- Application fees
- Deposits

### How to Apply

- **Phone:** **520-323-1708**
- **Days:** **Monday–Thursday** only — OFS admin office is currently closed on Fridays
