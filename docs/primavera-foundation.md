# The Primavera Foundation

The Primavera logo is a squash blossom — a reflection of *primavera* (spring) in Spanish, meaning renewal, rebirth, and new beginnings.

Primavera provides emergency shelter and emergency services across multiple programs in Tucson and Pima County.

## Mobile Outreach & Transportation Services

Launched in **early 2024** to "meet people where they are." Expanded to include transportation services to address the significant increase in unsheltered individuals and families.

> **Context:** The 2023 Point-in-Time (PIT) count indicated a **300% increase** in the population of unsheltered individuals experiencing homelessness between 2018 and 2023.

### Mobile Outreach

The team travels to locations in Pima County where unhoused individuals and families are located to form connections, complete/update assessments, and offer resources:

- Access to housing opportunities through the Coordinated Entry system
- HIP Drop-In Center and mail service registration
- Community mobile shower locations
- Access to medical care (walk-in clinics)
- Community organizations for detox or substance abuse complications
- Access to food and food pantries
- Clothing, laundry, mobile phones
- Pet health needs
- Additional needs within the community

During Outreach, items provided include: backpacks, tents, sleeping bags, snacks, water, pet supplies, hygiene kits, clothing, feminine hygiene products, shoes, and other useful items.

Mobile Outreach can also **transport participants to obtain services**.

### Transportation Services

Provided to current Primavera program participants. Transportation can include trips to:

- Department of Economic Security (DES)
- Educational opportunities
- Food bank / food supplies
- Housing
- Identification
- Job/employment
- Medical
- Shelter
- Social Security office
- Treatment center
- Obtaining birth certificate(s)

## Rent & Utility Assistance

**Call the Resource Center at 520-623-5111** (M–F, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.) to inquire about current funding and program availability.

> **Note:** If funding is available, you'll need to complete an intake appointment and turn in required documentation to determine eligibility.

### Eligibility

You may be eligible if:

- You are late to pay your rent or utilities
- You experienced a financial hardship causing inability to pay (e.g., job loss, major medical bill, car repair)
- Your income meets program qualifications
- You turn in required documentation within the allotted timeframe (determined at intake)
- Other eligibility requirements may apply

### Documentation Needed

- Photo ID for all adults
- Social Security card(s) for all household members
- Birth certificates or proof of citizenship for all household members
- Five-day notice from landlord or past due utility bills
- Proof of financial hardship/crisis
- Income verification for all household members for the last 30 days (employment, Social Security, unemployment, etc.)
- Other documents may be requested

## Rapid Rehousing

An integrated housing resource for families, couples, and individuals currently experiencing homelessness. Primavera participates in the **community-wide Coordinated Entry assessment and referral system** — prioritization of housing placement is handled the same way across the county.

You can complete a housing assessment in-person or over the phone at any **Community Coordinated Entry Access Point**, including the Primavera Drop-In Center at **702 S. 6th Ave**. Additional access points can be found at Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness (TPCH).

### Eligibility

Individuals and families who:

- Are experiencing homelessness
- Are willing to complete a comprehensive intake process if determined eligible
- Will actively participate in individual success planning and follow program rules

## Family Pathways Shelter Program

An **emergency shelter program for families of any configuration with minor children** currently experiencing homelessness.

- Rental units within the community provide housing for families
- Case management support with essential services focused on each specific family's goals
- Prevents multiple relocations
- As families progress, they transition through enrollment in another housing program or by sustaining the rental with their own income after the lease is transferred into their name

## Men's Shelter

Emergency shelter to men experiencing homelessness. Most begin with a **7-night stay** with the opportunity for extension up to a **total of 90 days**, if they qualify.

### Location & Contact

- **Address:** 200 E. Benson Highway, Tucson, AZ 85713
- **Phone:** **(520) 623-4300**
- **Reservations:** Call between **7–9 a.m. Monday to Friday (except Tuesdays)** to reserve a bed for that evening. If no space available, call frequently between 7–9 a.m. until a bed opens.

### Services

- Beds, laundry, meals, showers, hygiene supplies, clothing
- Resources, respite, case management support
- In-house medical and behavioral care through partners
- Information and referrals
- Education, art, and poetry workshops
- Day shelter (including cooling center)
- Computer lab

### Eligibility

- Unaccompanied male 18 years or older
- Stay clean and sober while on premises
- Do not have a level 2 or 3 sex offense conviction
- Willing to participate in case management

## Project Action for Veterans

Housing support services and **temporary financial assistance** to veterans and their families facing eviction or homelessness. Connects veterans to vital public and community resources.

**CARF Accredited** — three-year Commission on Accreditation of Rehabilitation Facilities accreditation running through early **2027**.

### Locations & Contact

| Office | Address | Phone |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Tucson** | 3504 S. 6th Ave., Ste. 150, Tucson, AZ 85713 | **(520) 308-3093** |
| **Rural (Sierra Vista)** | Inside Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, AZ 85635 | **(520) 302-6596** |

**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–Noon & 1–4:30 p.m. (closed Noon–1 p.m.)

### Process to Receive Veteran Support

1. **Call (520) 308-3093** for an initial phone interview
2. Staff member does preliminary eligibility assessment
3. If eligible, attend an intake appointment
4. To meet with a resource specialist, simply stop by

> Don't rule yourself out if you're missing documents — let staff know at the phone interview and they'll advise on obtaining them.

### Documentation

- Valid ID/driver's license
- DD214 or discharge status confirmed by Southern Arizona Veterans Administration + HINQ printout
- Social Security card
- Birth certificate(s) and school ID(s) for children
- Lease agreement
- Eviction notice
- Landlord contact information
- Copies of all utility bills
- Paystub or other proof of income for past 30 days

### Eligibility Criteria

- Must have served at least **one day of active duty** in the military, naval, air service, National Guard, or Merchant Marines
- Discharge or release status in any category **other than "dishonorable"**
- Must be experiencing homelessness, in foreclosure, or facing eviction
- Can be leaving transitional housing or a shelter, fleeing domestic violence, or recently discharged from a hospital or jail
- Household income at or below federal limits, based on household size, at time of intake
  - HUD Income Limit chart: https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/il.html
