# Coordinated Entry for Homeless Services

> **⚠️ See [tpch-need-help-guide-2026.md](tpch-need-help-guide-2026.md) for the most current access-point addresses, phones, and extensions** (December 2025). Several details below are superseded — notably: Our Family Services CE extension is now **x295** (not x103), La Frontera RAPP is at **STE 2**, and Interfaith Community Services has a direct line **(520) 477-0989**.

Coordinated Entry is the system used in Pima County for people experiencing homelessness (or youth at imminent risk) to get connected to housing services. **You complete one assessment at any location — no need to get on multiple lists.**

## Who It's For

You may benefit from Coordinated Entry if you have:

- Slept in a vehicle, outside, or shelter last night
- Stayed in a motel paid for by a church or charitable organization
- Survived domestic violence and are in need of housing

---

## How It Works

1. Complete a **Housing Questionnaire** with one of the Community Access Points
2. The research-based assessment identifies your unique needs and resources
3. You're placed on a **community-wide priority list** for housing assistance
4. Dozens of local programs accept referrals from this list
5. Referrals are based on:
   - The **severity of your needs**
   - The **availability of resources**
   - Your area / services available locally

Coordinated Entry is **accessible for people with disabilities**.

### Data Use

All assessments are centralized so the community can better:
- Track requests for assistance
- Track referrals and services provided
- Identify contributing factors to homelessness
- Plan for housing needs

---

## What to Expect — Phone Assessment

1. **You may be asked to make an appointment** or forwarded to voicemail. If this happens, you may call another Access Point or wait for a callback. They will return your call **within 1 business day**.
2. **You may be referred** to a different agency or phone number on the list.
3. **Release of Information** — you'll be read an ROI that normally requires a signature. You will only be assessed if you **verbally agree** to it.
4. **Check in every 30 days** with your assessor to stay on the priority list. Provide updates to your contact information or circumstances.

---

## Grievances

If you believe you have been discriminated against or treated unfairly by the TPCH Coordinated Entry System — or if you're a service provider who believes TPCH has violated its Coordinated Entry policies and procedures — you can complete and submit a grievance through TPCH.
