# Sullivan Jackson Employment Center (SJEC)

**Employment & Resource Assistance for Youth & Adults Experiencing Homelessness**

The Sullivan Jackson Employment Center (SJEC) opened its doors in **1986** to respond to the employment and training needs of homeless men and women seeking to enter the workforce. SJEC has been a pioneer in developing effective approaches to assist homeless individuals and families seeking to become self-sufficient.

SJEC's clients consistently obtain and retain **full-time, unsubsidized employment above minimum wage**.

---

## Program Philosophy

SJEC believes that clients must be **job ready** — have all of their basic needs met — before they can focus on career choices and learn the techniques that will result in employment. In this regard, SJEC is unlike other employment and training programs.

Clients are enrolled in an **Integrated Individual Service Strategy program** that results in:
- A **Case Plan**
- An **Employability Development Plan**

See the latest Sullivan Jackson Employment Center Program Brochure (PDF) for more information.

---

## Program Eligibility

SJEC does not target a particular segment of the homeless population. SJEC accepts all individuals — men, women, youth, and veterans — who meet the basic program requirements:

- Homeless
- Unemployed
- Job-willing
- Referred by another community-based agency

---

## How the Program Works

1. **Sheltering first.** Each client is sheltered as the first step of program participation.
2. **Case management & linkages.** Using case management practices, additional needs are identified and services are provided as needed (usually through linkages with other agencies).
3. **Employability Skills training.** Once basic needs are met, clients enter a **two-week Employability Skills training program**.
4. **Structured job search.** Clients dedicate themselves to a structured job search program with the assistance and review of case management and job development staff.
5. **Transition support.** When employment is obtained, SJEC continues to support the client's transition to independent living. The program may provide employment tools, uniforms, and specialized footwear when needed.
6. **Aftercare Program.** Clients may participate in SJEC's aftercare program for **up to one year** following employment. The program focuses on early and prompt intervention to prevent loss of employment and assistance in adjusting to work and independent living.

SJEC also helps clients with an array of supportive services, including **educational opportunities**, usually provided by partner agencies.

## How to Schedule an Appointment

If you are willing to work, want to become self-sufficient, and want to increase your employability skills:

- **Call:** **520-724-7300**
- Schedule an intake appointment with a case manager.
- **Note:** Due to scheduled appointments, **no intakes are possible on a walk-in basis**.
