# Youth On Their Own (YOTO)

Supports the high school graduation and continued success of **youth experiencing homelessness in grades 6–12** who are enrolled in a public, private, charter, or alternative school in Pima County.

## Location & Contact

- **Address:** 2525 N Country Club Rd, Tucson, AZ 85716
- **Phone:** (520) 293-1136
- **Website:** **yotoaz.org**

## How to Apply

- **Online:** Complete an application at **yotoaz.org**
- **In school:** Contact a YOTO School Liaison (principal, teacher, counselor, etc.)

---

## Eligibility — Who YOTO Serves

To be eligible, students must be:

- Enrolled in a Pima County **middle or high school (grades 6–12)** and **under age 22**
- **Unaccompanied** through no fault/choice of their own — not in the physical custody of a parent or long-term legal guardian, and without a stable home
- Homeless per the **McKinney-Vento definition**
- Without the care of a long-term parent or long-term guardian

---

## How YOTO Serves — Three Program Components

### 1. Financial Assistance

**Monthly Stipend Program:**

- Students are eligible to receive a **monthly stipend of up to $350**
- Provides a safety net for managing daily needs
- Eligibility requires **regularly attending school**

### 2. Basic Human Needs

**Mini Mall (Basic Needs):**

- Free basic needs items including:
  - Food
  - Hygiene items
  - Household goods
  - School supplies
- Visit in-person, or request delivery to the student's school
- Many items are donations from community members, organizations, and businesses

**Transportation:**

- Sun Tran is currently free to all riders; **YOTO will provide monthly bus passes** if that changes
- **Bicycles** are also provided for transportation to school, work, and healthcare appointments

### 3. Guidance

Referrals to social service and healthcare agencies to ensure students get the support required to graduate. Services available through partner agencies, organizations, and businesses include:

- Housing Referrals
- Personal Counseling
- One-on-One Success Coaching
- Medical, Dental, and Vision Care
- Employment Opportunities
- Community Involvement Activities

---

## Alumni Program — Life After High School

For most YOTO youth, without family stability or parental guidance, planning a future career feels impossible. The **YOTO Alumni Team** provides guidance, mentoring, and training in:

- Pursuing possible educational scholarship opportunities
- Assessing skills to determine possible career paths after high school
- Preparation for job placement — application, resume, and interview
