# Esperanza En Escalante (Veterans Housing)

**Transitional and permanent supportive housing for homeless Veterans and their families** on a 14-acre campus in southeast Tucson. Serving Tucson Veterans for **36 years** — "Housing ★ Support ★ Honor." The mission: a clear path from crisis to stability and independent living.

## Programs

### Transitional Housing
Short-term, structured housing providing a safe environment for Veterans to get back on their feet while addressing personal, economic, and health challenges.

### Permanent Supportive Housing
Long-term, stable housing with subsidized rent and ongoing case management for a sustainable, independent future.

### Integrated Services
Access to mental health care, substance abuse treatment, and job assistance to address the root causes of homelessness. "Mission Forward" hiring initiatives partner with local employers to place Veterans in meaningful work.

### Accommodations
- Individual units for men and women
- Family options
- Fully accessible units for Veterans with disabilities

---

## Eligibility

- Served in any U.S. military branch with **honorable discharge**
- Currently **homeless or at risk** of homelessness
- Willing to maintain a **substance-free** living environment
- Able to participate actively in support programs
- Capable of independent living while transitioning to permanent housing in Pima County

## Intake

Apply by phone at **(520) 675-2707** or email info@eeeveterans.org. Referrals require the applicant's name, birthdate, Social Security number, and reason for referral.

## Program Fees

Income-based; fees **do not exceed 30% of applicable income**, capped at **$300/month (single)** or **$400/month (family)**.
