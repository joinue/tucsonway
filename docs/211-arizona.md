# 2-1-1 Arizona — Information and Referral

A statewide information and referral service that connects Arizonans to thousands of community resources. For more than **55 years**, Arizona families and human service workers have relied on Information & Referral.

## History & Operator

- Founded in **1964** as Community Information and Referral Services
- Incorporated as a private, nonprofit 501(c)(3) in **1979**
- Currently operated by **Solari Crisis & Human Services** (acquired in 2017)
- Pima County office is co-located at Our Family Services: **2590 N Alvernon Way**

## Availability

- **24 hours per day, 7 days per week, every day of the year**
- Live-operator service is available at all times in **English and Spanish**
- Assistance is available in **other languages via real-time interpreter services**

---

## What 2-1-1 Helps With

Operators help individuals and families find resources locally and statewide, including:

- Supplemental Food and Nutrition Programs
- Shelter and Housing Options
- Utilities Assistance
- Emergency Information and Disaster Relief
- Employment and Education Opportunities
- Services for Veterans
- Healthcare, vaccination, and health epidemic information
- Addiction Prevention and Rehabilitation Programs
- Re-entry help for ex-offenders
- Support groups for individuals with mental illnesses or special needs
- A safe, confidential path out of physical and/or emotional domestic violence
- Legal assistance, GED classes, computer classes, counseling, parenting classes
- Donation pick-ups, volunteer opportunities, animal services

## Three Ways to Find Services

### 1. Dial **2-1-1** within Arizona

The 2-1-1 helpline has returned to **live answer all day, every day**.

- Call to speak to an information specialist for referrals.
- Call anytime to listen to recorded information about the most commonly requested community resources.

### 2. Search the Online Database

2-1-1 Arizona is a **statewide database of community resources**. On the website:
- Choose your county on the left
- Select a service from the most commonly requested categories
- Organizations can also **add themselves to the resource database**

### 3. Purchase a Directory

The **2022 edition of the Directory of Community Resources for Pima County** is available for purchase for **$40**.

- In person: Office at **2590 N Alvernon Way**
- Online purchase available
