# AZ Pet Project — ADOH Flex Funds

Flexible funding (ADOH = Arizona Department of Housing) administered by AZ Pet Project. **Funding is statewide** — anyone in Arizona can apply.

## Who It's a Good Fit For

Participants who:

- Are facing **eviction due to unpaid rent**
- Need assistance paying **pet rent or pet deposits**
- Need assistance with **utilities, food, transportation, veterinary care, and/or employment expenses** to preserve rent funds
- Need assistance with **mortgages or critical home repairs** to keep the home livable (A/C, roof, etc.)
- Need assistance with **car repairs to retain employment**
- Are experiencing homelessness and need assistance with **deposits or rent to regain housing** — funds may also be used for **moving expenses and critical furniture**

---

## Eligibility & Limits

- **Geography:** Statewide (any Arizona resident)
- **Income:** At or below **80% of the Area Median Income (AMI)**
- **Award limit:** **$3,000 per household**, one-time assistance
- **Documentation:** Proof of financial need (eviction notices, past-due statements, etc.)

## How to Apply

Submit the **Request Assistance Form** (via AZ Pet Project).
