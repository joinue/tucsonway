About Tucson Way

Tucson Way is an independent informational directory that helps people in Tucson and Pima County find shelter and recovery resources.

Tucson Way is not affiliated with the organizations listed here. We do not provide shelter, funding, intake, or any direct services.

Privacy

This site does not require sign-in, does not collect personal information, and does not use tracking analytics that profile visitors. Your location, if you choose to share it, is used only on your device to sort the directory.

About the listings

Information is provided as-is and may change. Please call ahead to confirm hours, eligibility, and availability. Verified providers are sourced from the City of Tucson's Housing & Community Development department.

Updates and corrections

If a listing is out of date or missing, please notify the City of Tucson or the provider directly. They are the authoritative source.

In an emergency, call 911.
