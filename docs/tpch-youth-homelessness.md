# TPCH — Ending Youth Homelessness in Tucson/Pima County

Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness (TPCH) is on a mission to **prevent and end youth homelessness in Pima County**, working to elevate youth power and choice in decision-making at the individual, organizational, and system level.

> See also: [tpch-continuum-of-care.md](tpch-continuum-of-care.md) for the parent organization.

---

## National Initiatives (Selected 2019)

TPCH was selected to participate in two national initiatives to address youth homelessness in 2019:

- **HUD's Youth Homelessness Demonstration Program (YHDP)** — The Tucson/Pima County Continuum of Care was awarded **$4.558 million** to accelerate community efforts to prevent and end youth homelessness.
- **A Way Home America Grand Challenge** — Partnership with Federal technical assistance providers and **nine other communities** across the nation to end homelessness among youth of color and LGBTQ+ youth by 2022.

---

## Vision

We envision communities and systems throughout Tucson/Pima County in which the **causes of youth homelessness are disrupted** and its solutions are **permanent**. This requires radical systems change built on intersectional equity and youth power. In these systems, young people drive individual housing solutions and hold equal power at all levels of community decision-making.

## Youth Action Committee (YAC)

A collective of **youth leaders age 24 and younger** with current or past experience of homelessness or housing instability.

YAC members:
- Guide the implementation of the Coordinated Community Plan
- Drive continuous quality improvement strategies
- Are decision-makers on YHDP funding and planning priorities

## YHDP Grantees — Funded Projects (2020 Launch)

Six new housing and supportive service projects, totaling **more than $4.1M over two years**, dramatically increasing housing/services for young people experiencing homelessness, with a focus on racial equity, LGBTQ+ inclusivity, and cross-system coordination.

### 1. Youth Care Navigation & Diversion (Goodwill Pairing)

- **Organization:** Goodwill of Southern Arizona
- **Grant:** $295,398
- **Description:** Goodwill METRO/REC pairs peer outreach and advocacy delivered by young people with lived experience of homelessness with comprehensive educational and employment support services. Services include high school/GED and college enrollment & completion assistance, employment readiness, job training, and connections to employment.

### 2. Youth Care Navigation & Diversion (Our Family + UA SIROW)

- **Organizations:** Our Family Services and University of Arizona Southwest Institute for Research on Women (SIROW)
- **Grant:** $658,272
- **Description:** Youth Care provides housing and service navigation support by linking young people to crisis and long-term housing options, mainstream benefits, and other community services. Provides flexible funding to assist youth to rapidly resolve experiences of homelessness through family re-engagement, host homes, and other strategies.

### 3. Bread and Roses Crisis Transitional Housing

- **Organizations:** Old Pueblo Community Services (OPCS) and Southern Arizona AIDS Foundation (SAAF)
- **Grant:** $864,000
- **Description:** Short-term transitional housing specifically designed to center and respond to the unique needs of **LGBTQ+ young adults**. Provides a safe, restorative environment to help young people heal from trauma and transition to family reunification, longer-term housing services, or self-sufficiency.

### 4. CBI YHDP Rapid Rehousing Project

- **Organization:** Community Bridges, Inc.
- **Grant:** $819,492
- **Description:** Up to **36 months** of permanent housing support, average stay 12 months. Services include rental assistance plus behavioral health and substance abuse services, case management, education and employment linkage, and wraparound support.

### 5. New Hope Rapid Rehousing Project

- **Organization:** Our Family Services
- **Grant:** $966,509
- **Description:** Permanent housing support for up to **36 months**, average stay 12 months. Services include rental assistance plus life skills, education and employment linkages, counseling, case management, and wraparound support for single youth, young couples, and parenting young adults.

### 6. Transitions Permanent Supportive Housing Project

- **Organization:** Community Partnership of Southern Arizona
- **Grant:** $504,000
- **Description:** **Non-time-limited** rental assistance and supportive services for young people with disabilities and compounding supportive service needs. Includes housing, integrated health and behavioral healthcare, education and employment services, and case management.
