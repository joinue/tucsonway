# The Salvation Army — Hospitality House

24/7 emergency shelter located near downtown Tucson, providing both short- and long-term housing assistance.

## Facility

- **Open:** 24/7
- **Address:** 1002 N. Main Ave, Tucson, AZ (near downtown Tucson)
- **Phone:** (520) 624-5411
- **Capacity:** **114-bed facility** with separate accommodations for men and women
- **Families:** **4 family apartments**; motel vouchers may be available to assist additional families

### Amenities

- Safe, clean environment
- Laundry
- Showers
- Television
- Dining (**two meals per day**)
- Access to vital programs and services

---

## Shelter Programs

Programs are targeted to emergency housing needs, with both short- and long-term options:

| Program | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Emergency shelter (short term) | **21 days** |
| Transitional assistance (long term) | **4 months** |

### Life-Transforming Services

- Professional Case Management (in-house and through referrals)
- Health & Medical Assistance
- Agency Referrals & Support Groups
- Employment Services (through referrals)
- Life Skills Guidance & Training (through referrals)
- Worship Services and Bible Study
- 24-Hour Emergency Assistance

### On-Site Activities for Guests

- Peer Support Services
- Case Management
- Bible Study
- Sunday worship services

---

## Seasonal & Crisis Outreach

During stressful times of the year and in response to crises, The Hospitality House also engages in additional outreach for the homeless:

- Coat and blanket distribution
- Emergency shelter during extreme temperatures and weather
- Water distribution
- Toiletries
- Sun relief items

---

## Annual Impact

- **88,000+ meals** provided annually by The Salvation Army Tucson
- **27,000+ people** helped annually

Services are provided in collaboration with several other agencies.
