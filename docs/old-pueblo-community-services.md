# Old Pueblo Community Services (OPCS)

A nonprofit (501(c)(3)) homelessness services organization based in Tucson. OPCS operates housing, supportive services, and the Tucson Homeless Work Program, with a **Housing First** philosophy.

## Programs

### Low Barrier Bridge Housing (Housing First)

OPCS's flagship Housing First Homeless Assistance program. Serves homeless individuals and families that **do not meet the requirements of traditional shelter**.

**Unlike traditional mass shelter, residents have:**
- Semi-private rooms within units
- Units with a kitchen and refrigerator
- The ability to secure their own belongings
- Access to an on-site laundry facility

**Once in housing, residents receive:**
- Assistance applying for entitlements
- Case management
- Help obtaining permanent housing
- Supportive services including:
  - Substance abuse counseling
  - Employment coaching

**Housing First approach:** Residents are **NOT required to be stable in their recovery** from addictions or mental health conditions before entering. Acceptance is **prioritized by level of vulnerability**.

### Veteran Assistance Programs

OPCS veteran programs are operated **in partnership with the Veterans Administration, the City of Tucson, and other community partners.**

**OPCS welcomes:**
- Veterans with **criminal justice history**
- Veterans **discharging from the hospital, detox, or treatment**

The VA Hospital provides immediate and effective detoxification services and a full range of medical, psychiatric, and rehabilitative care services. OPCS supports these interventions by offering:

- Assistance with ID, birth certificates, food, and clothing
- **Structured housing free of drugs and alcohol**
- Case management
- Substance abuse counseling and recovery support
- Employment supports
- Bus passes
- 12-Step and SMART Recovery
- Referral to other community-based services
- Life skills classes
- Assistance with permanent housing upon completion

#### Eligibility & Referral

**For veterans with service-connected benefits:**
- Eligibility and referral for OPCS Veteran Assistance programs is **determined by the Tucson VA Homeless Clinic**
- **Location:** SAVAHCS, **3601 S 6th Ave., Building 90, Tucson, AZ**

**For veterans NOT eligible for VA benefits** (e.g., due to discharge status):
- May apply for **OPCS emergency/bridge housing** directly

> **Note:** The veteran-specific structured housing is **dry (drug- and alcohol-free)**, distinct from OPCS's general Low Barrier Bridge Housing which is Housing First / non-abstinence-based.

## What Makes OPCS Distinct

- **Low-barrier** model — no sobriety or recovery requirement to enter housing
- **Semi-private rooms with kitchens** — closer to apartment living than mass shelter
- **Vulnerability-prioritized intake** — most vulnerable served first
- Operates the **only** non-governmental work program of its kind in Tucson (HWP)
