# Emerge — Center Against Domestic Abuse

Domestic abuse support and shelter services. **Call for directions for shelter** — location is not publicly listed for safety reasons.

## Hotlines

- **24-hour, multi-lingual hotline:** **(520) 795-4266**
- **24-hour, toll-free hotline:** **(888) 428-0101**

Call the hotline number for immediate help. The hotline acts as the **gateway to all of Emerge's services**.

Family, friends, and community members who are worried for a loved one can also call for support.

## Who Can Call

- Individuals experiencing domestic abuse
- Family members concerned about a loved one
- Friends concerned about a loved one
- Community members seeking guidance
