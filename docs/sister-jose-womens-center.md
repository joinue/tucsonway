## Why It Matters

A growing number of older women are experiencing homelessness for the first time. Rising housing costs and inflation are making it increasingly difficult to remain housed on a fixed income, leaving many without options or support.

Life on the streets is unpredictable and unsafe. Women often lose what little they have — personal belongings, identification, medications, and meaningful items that connect them to their past — making it even harder to regain stability.

## Programs

A continuum of care built around **three core areas**, in a compassionate, trauma-informed environment where every woman is treated with dignity, respect, and care.

### 1. Core Services — Meeting Immediate Needs with Dignity

Essential daily resources in a safe and welcoming environment. For many, this is the first step toward stability and healing.

- Nutritious meals (breakfast, lunch, and to-go dinners)
- Hot showers and hygiene supplies
- Clean clothing and on-site laundry
- Mail services, phone access, and internet
- Safe indoor space for rest, including cooling and heat relief

Additional access to:

- On-site medical and behavioral health providers
- Wellness activities and community support
- Crisis de-escalation and compassionate care

### 2. Overnight Shelter — Safe, Stable, and **Pet-Inclusive**

A safe place for women — and their companion pets — to rest, recover, and begin rebuilding their lives.

Each guest receives:

- A dedicated bed, storage space, and clean linens
- 24/7 access to a secure and stable environment
- A supportive, trauma-informed setting that promotes healing

Additional support:

- Supper Club community meals
- Extreme weather relief (heat and cold)
- Opportunities for engagement and community connection

**54 beds** available nightly — more than just a place to sleep; a sense of safety, belonging, and stability critical for women emerging from trauma and homelessness.

### CREATE Program (Residential Life Skills + Job Readiness)

A residential program where women can **learn life skills and job readiness while receiving a stipend**. Call the main line (520-909-3905) for more information about CREATE.

### 3. Case Navigation — Personalized Pathways to Stability

Individualized, one-on-one support using a **Housing First, low-barrier approach**.

Case Navigators:

- Co-create personalized health and housing plans
- Help women access medical, behavioral health, and social services
- Assist with employment readiness, resumes, and job placement
- Support obtaining vital documents (IDs, Social Security cards, etc.)
- Provide financial assistance for housing, transportation, and basic needs
- Build trusted relationships — the foundation for long-term progress and lasting change

## Approach — More Than Services, a Community of Care

Sister José understands that homelessness is complex. The approach focuses on both immediate relief and long-term stability:

- Provide a safe, consistent place for women to rest and recover
- Integrate housing, healthcare, and supportive services
- Reduce barriers so every woman can access care
- Support women in building resilience and self-sufficiency
