# Youth Need Help Directory

A list of resources for **employment, health, housing, and necessary services** in Tucson / Pima County, oriented toward youth and young adults. Sourced from TPCH's Youth Need Help Directory.

> Many organizations listed here have their own detailed files in this docs folder — see cross-references where they exist.

---

## Housing & Shelter

### Gospel Rescue Mission — 28th St Campus
- **Address:** 312 W. 28th St, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 740-1501 ext. 7212
- **Services:** Shelter, Employment, Housing, Recovery, Outreach
- **Admission:** Intake from 4–5:15 PM
- See also: [gospel-rescue-mission-center-of-opportunity.md](gospel-rescue-mission-center-of-opportunity.md)

### Gospel Rescue Mission — Miracle Mile Campus
- **Address:** 707 W. Miracle Mile, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 740-1501 ext. 7101
- **Services:** Shelter, Employment, Housing, Recovery, Outreach
- **Admission:** Intake by appointment — call to schedule

### Our Family Services
- **Address:** 2590 N. Alvernon Way, Tucson, AZ
- **Youth 12–17:** (520) 320-5122
- **Young Adults 18–24:** (520) 323-1708 ext. 103
- **Youth services:** Necessities, home-cooked meals, enrollment in school, reunification with families if possible, safe shelter
- **Young Adult services:** Emergency housing, housing services, connection to employment and education programs, counseling, legal services
- **Admission:** Call for admission
- See also: [our-family-services.md](our-family-services.md)

### Primavera Men's Shelter
- **Address:** 200 E. Benson Highway, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 623-4300
- **Services:** Showers, laundry, drinks, clothing, cots, personal supplies, case management
- **Admission:** Call at 9 AM to reserve a bed for that evening. Unaccompanied males 18+ only, must be sober, no level 2 or 3 sex offense convictions.
- See also: [primavera-foundation.md](primavera-foundation.md)

### Salvation Army Hospitality House
- **Address:** 1002 N. Main Ave, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 624-5411
- **Services:** 24/7 emergency shelter, laundry, showers, television, dining (2 meals/day)
- See also: [salvation-army-hospitality-house.md](salvation-army-hospitality-house.md)

### CODAC Pima Youth Shelter
- **Address:** 2050 E. 8th St, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 495-5537
- **Services:** Therapy, support, shelter, substance abuse support
- **Eligibility:** Youth **12–17 who are already receiving CODAC services**
- **Notes:** Not open intake — must already be in CODAC's system

### Sister José Women's Center
- **Address:** 1050 S. Park Ave, Tucson, AZ
- **Phone:** (520) 909-3905
- **Services:** Meals, showers, laundry, clothing, hygiene items, blankets, WiFi, **pet-friendly overnight housing**, **CREATE Program (Residential Program for Learning Life Skills)**
- **Admission:** Women only. Call for more information about CREATE (residential life skills + job readiness program with stipend).
- See also: [sister-jose-womens-center.md](sister-jose-womens-center.md)
