Tucson Compass is a free, private web app that helps anyone facing homelessness, or at risk of losing their home, find the right help fast. No app store, no sign-up, no catch. It works on any phone.

What it does

Find Your Fit

Answer a few private questions in about a minute and get a personalized list of services that match the person's real situation.

Full directory

Searchable, filterable listings for shelter, food, recovery, veterans, youth, families, and more, grouped by need.

Map & directions

See nearby locations, tap for turn-by-turn directions, and reach domestic-violence hotlines that keep their address private.

Resources & links

Official phone numbers and websites: coordinated entry, cooling centers, live bed availability, benefits, and crisis help.

Works offline

Once opened, key pages and the map keep working without signal, built for prepaid phones and spotty data.

English & Español

Switch the entire site between English and Spanish with one tap, so language is never a barrier to getting help.

About the listings: Tucson Compass is an independent, free informational directory. Information is compiled from public sources, including the City of Tucson, Pima County, TPCH Coordinated Entry, 211 Arizona, and the Community Food Bank, and may change. Always call ahead to confirm hours and availability. It is not affiliated with the organizations listed and does not provide services directly.
