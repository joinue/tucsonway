# Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness (TPCH)

A coalition of community and faith-based organizations, homeless service providers, government entities, behavioral health agencies, and individuals committed to **ending homelessness and addressing related issues** in Tucson and Pima County.

> **Important:** TPCH does **not provide direct services**. If you are in need of housing services, call **311**.

---

## Goals

- End homelessness in Tucson and Pima County, Arizona
- Provide leadership, expertise, and advice to local planning and funding bodies on issues that impact services to people experiencing homelessness
- Increase community awareness and support for people experiencing homelessness and homeless issues
- Develop systems-level programming and coordinate agencies for implementation
- Act as the **U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) Continuum of Care (CoC)** for the geographic area of the City of Tucson and Pima County

---

## Our Work

The TPCH Board of Directors and committees work year-round to:

- Identify service and resource gaps and significant trends in homeless populations through evaluation
- Develop priorities, based on these trends and gaps, for improving the service network
- Identify and apply for funding opportunities aligned with TPCH priorities
- Strategically plan to end homelessness in Pima County

---

## Structure

TPCH is comprised of an **elected board** and **committees** that focus on various parts of the homeless response system. TPCH convenes and coordinates community partners to create a forum for community consensus on needs and homeless service delivery.

### Continuum of Care (CoC) Board

The Continuum of Care Board is an independent body comprised of community stakeholders committed to furthering TPCH's work to prevent and end homelessness in Tucson and throughout Pima County. The CoC Board serves as the **primary decision-making body** for TPCH and oversees regional planning and funding activities.

## General Council — Membership Body

The TPCH General Council is the collaboration's membership body. It meets **quarterly on the second Thursday** of:

- February
- May (Annual Meeting)
- August
- November

### Two Types of Members

**General Members** — anyone interested in ending homelessness and related issues who attends meetings and engages in discussion. TPCH welcomes a wide variety of perspectives, and individuals from aligned services are encouraged to join.

**Voting Members** — members who regularly participate in both General Council and committee meetings and submit a membership form. Voting Members:

- Make and second motions
- Vote on motions
- Elect members of the CoC Board and committees to open seats at the Annual Meeting

### How to Become a Voting Member

1. Complete and submit a **membership or renewal form**
2. Attend **two consecutive TPCH General Council meetings**
3. Retain voting privileges by:
   - Continuing to attend at least **one committee meeting per quarter**
   - Attending at least every other General Council meeting (do not miss two consecutive General Council meetings)

To start the process, begin attending meetings and fill out the membership form at **www.tpch.net/join**.

### Annual Renewal (Adopted 2023)

All General Council members must **renew organizational memberships annually between July 1 and September 30**. Check the membership roster to see whether your organization's membership needs renewal. If not listed, the renewal wasn't captured and a new form must be submitted.
