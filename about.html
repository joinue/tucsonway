About Tucson Compass

Tucson Compass is a free directory of shelter, recovery, food, medical, and other support services in Tucson and Pima County. It is independent and does not require sign-up.

What you can do here

  • Find Your Fit. Answer a few private questions and get a list of services that fit your situation. Takes about a minute. Your answers stay on your device.
  • Browse the directory. Search by name or service. Filter by who a provider serves: women, men, families, youth, veterans, LGBTQ+, domestic violence survivors, and more.
  • See locations on a map. View nearby providers, tap a pin to call or get directions, and sort by what is closest to you.
  • Reach crisis help fast. 911, 988 for mental health, 211 for info and referral, and the 24-hour domestic violence hotline are linked from every page.
  • Use it in English or Spanish. Switch languages anytime. Save the site to your phone's home screen and it will work without a signal.

Tucson Compass is not affiliated with the organizations listed here. We do not provide shelter, funding, intake, or any direct services. We point you to the providers who do.

Privacy

This site does not require sign-in, does not collect personal information, and does not use tracking analytics that profile visitors. Your location, if you choose to share it, is used only on your device to sort the directory. Your Find Your Fit answers never leave your phone.

About the listings

Information is provided as-is and may change. Please call ahead to confirm hours, eligibility, and availability. Listings are compiled from public sources, including the City of Tucson, the Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness (TPCH), Pima County, and the providers' own websites.

Updates and corrections

If a listing is out of date or missing, please notify the City of Tucson or the provider directly. They are the authoritative source.
